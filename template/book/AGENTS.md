# Book agent instructions

These rules belong to this book. They supplement the hub's agent-neutral instructions and the
editorial profile; they do not replace them.

## Roles

- **Owner:** approves the brief, outline, pilot, merges and releases.
- **Implementer:** changes one scoped chapter or component and records evidence.
- **Reviewer:** a different person or agent; reviews the exact commit and re-runs the checks.

Never self-review or self-merge.

## Workflow

1. Read the approved brief and outline before writing.
2. Keep one chapter or one small batch per change.
3. Preserve cross-references, figure and table numbering, and checklist IDs.
4. Run the book's fast checks on every commit and the full build before review.
5. Record commands actually run; mark everything else as not run.
6. Update the change record and status in the same change.

## Content

- English, vendor-neutral, decision-oriented.
- No invented sources, standards or evidence.
- Preserve licensing and attribution; complete a third-party rights review before importing material.
- Follow the pinned editorial profile; record any exception with owner approval.
